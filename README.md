## Overview

**Anvay** is a modern cloud ERP platform designed specifically for growing retailers, distributors, wholesalers, and manufacturers. It centralizes sales billing, multi-location inventory, and statutory compliance into a clean, intuitive web experience.

---

## Key Capabilities

* **Omnichannel Point of Sale (POS):** Fast barcode scanning, multi-counter billing, thermal receipt printing, and digital invoice generation.
* **Intelligent Tax Handling:** Automated place-of-supply tax calculations across inter-state (IGST) and intra-state (CGST + SGST) transactions.
* **Multi-Location Inventory:** Real-time stock tracking across multiple warehouses, branch transfers, batch tracking, and low-inventory alerts.
* **Instant Digital Delivery:** Automated invoice dispatch via WhatsApp and email, complete with secure online verification links.
* **Frictionless Migration:** Built-in data import tools allowing businesses to easily bring over customer ledgers and product catalogs from legacy systems.
